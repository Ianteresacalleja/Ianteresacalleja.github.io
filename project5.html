Oye Calle Ocho

Oye Calle Ocho is a Cuban Cafe based in Little Havana, celebrating a love of coffee and cuban culture. This identity system and packaging is an exploration of cuban iconography, typography and illustration, embracing a kind of rustic disorder and jubilation unique to cuban design.
